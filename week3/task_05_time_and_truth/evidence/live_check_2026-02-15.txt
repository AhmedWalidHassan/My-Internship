Q: How many PTO days can I carry over into next year?
A: Under HR-POL-004 v2.0, effective 2025-01-01, unused PTO of up to 5 days may be carried into the next calendar year; any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31. However, under the new HR-POL-004 v3.0, effective 2026-03-01, only up to 3 days of unused PTO may be carried over, with any excess forfeited on December 31 and carried-over days required to be used by March 31 of that year. A transition rule for 2026 states that balances carried from 2025 under v2.0 (up to 5 days) are honoured in full but must also be used by March 31, 2026.
