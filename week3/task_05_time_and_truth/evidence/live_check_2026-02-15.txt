Q: How many PTO days can I carry over into next year?
A: Under HR-POL-004 v2.0, effective 2025-01-01, unused PTO of up to 5 days may be carried into the next calendar year; any balance above 5 days is forfeited on December 31. However, under the new policy (HR-POL-004 v3.0), effective 2026-03-01, unused PTO of up to 3 days may be carried into the next calendar year; any balance above 3 days is forfeited on December 31. There is a transition rule for 2026 only: balances carried from 2025 under version 2.0 (up to 5 days) are honoured in full but must also be used by March 31, 2026.
