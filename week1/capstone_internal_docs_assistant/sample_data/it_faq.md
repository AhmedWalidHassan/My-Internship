# Northwind IT FAQ

## Password Policy
Passwords must be at least 14 characters long and are rotated every 180 days. You cannot reuse your last 10 passwords.
Multi-factor authentication (MFA) is mandatory for all accounts.

## VPN Access
Use the NorthLink VPN through the GlobalConnect client whenever you access internal systems off-site.
If the VPN will not connect, restart the GlobalConnect client first, then check status.northwind.example for outages.

## Laptops and Hardware
Laptops are refreshed every 3 years. Request a replacement through the ServiceDesk portal.
Lost or stolen devices must be reported to security@northwind.example within 24 hours.

## Software Requests
Approved applications are listed in the Self Service app. Requests for new software go through a security review that takes 5 business days.

## Getting Help
The IT helpdesk is reachable at extension 4357 (HELP) or helpdesk@northwind.example, weekdays from 7:00 to 19:00 ET.
Priority-1 outages are handled 24/7 by the on-call engineer at extension 4999.

## Wi-Fi
Employees connect to NW-Secure. Guests use NW-Guest; its password rotates weekly and is posted at reception.
