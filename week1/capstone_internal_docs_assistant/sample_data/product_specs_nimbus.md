# Nimbus N2 Autonomous Mobile Robot — Product Specification

## Overview
The Nimbus N2 is an autonomous mobile robot (AMR) for moving pallets and totes in warehouses.

## Performance
- Payload: 250 kg
- Maximum speed: 1.8 m/s
- Maximum climbable slope: 5%

## Battery
The N2 has a 48 V, 40 Ah battery that gives about 10 hours of runtime. Charging from 0 to 80% takes 90 minutes.

## Navigation
Navigation uses SLAM with a 360° LIDAR that has a 25 m range.
A single Nimbus Fleet Manager can coordinate up to 50 robots.

## Commercial
Ingress protection is IP42. List price is $32,500 and the warranty is 12 months.
