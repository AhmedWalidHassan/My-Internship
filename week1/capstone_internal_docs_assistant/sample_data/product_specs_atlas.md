# Atlas A7 Collaborative Robot Arm — Product Specification

## Overview
The Atlas A7 is a 6-axis collaborative robot arm designed for machine tending, assembly and small-parts handling.

## Performance
- Payload: 7 kg
- Reach: 850 mm
- Repeatability: ±0.02 mm
- Weight: 21 kg

## Power and Connectivity
- Supply: 48 V DC, 350 W peak
- Interfaces: EtherCAT and Modbus TCP

## Safety and Environment
The A7 is force-limited: it stops when contact force exceeds 140 N.
Ingress protection is IP54 and the operating temperature range is 0 to 45 °C.

## Commercial
List price is $18,900 and the warranty is 24 months.
