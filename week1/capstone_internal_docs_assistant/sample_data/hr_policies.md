# Northwind Robotics — HR Policies

## Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 20 days of PTO per year (about 1.67 days per month). After 5 years of service the allowance rises to 25 days.
Up to 5 unused PTO days carry over into the next year; any remaining balance expires on March 31.
PTO requests of 3 or more consecutive days must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through Workday.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers receive 16 weeks of fully paid leave. Secondary caregivers receive 6 weeks of fully paid leave.
Leave must be taken within 12 months of the birth or adoption. Employees become eligible after 6 months of employment.

## Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 sick days per year. Sick days do not carry over.
A doctor's note is required after 3 consecutive sick days.

## Remote and Hybrid Work
Northwind uses a hybrid model: employees are expected in the office at least on Tuesdays and Thursdays.
Fully remote arrangements require approval from a Vice President.
Remote-eligible employees receive a one-time $500 home-office stipend and a $50 monthly internet reimbursement.

## Expense Reimbursement
Submit expenses within 30 days through Concur. Receipts are required for any expense over $25.
Meals while traveling are capped at $75 per day.
Flights under 6 hours are booked in economy; flights over 6 hours may be booked in premium economy.

## Performance Reviews
Reviews happen twice a year, in June and December, and use a 1-5 rating scale.
Promotion decisions are made in the January cycle.
