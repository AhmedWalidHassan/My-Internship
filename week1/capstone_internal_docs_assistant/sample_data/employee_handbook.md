# Northwind Robotics — Employee Handbook

## About Northwind
Northwind Robotics was founded in 2016 and is headquartered in Pittsburgh, Pennsylvania, with additional offices in Munich and Austin.
The company has roughly 850 employees. Our mission is to make automation accessible to every workshop.

## Working Hours
Standard full-time work is 40 hours per week. Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 local time; outside core hours schedules are flexible.

## Benefits
Health insurance starts on the first day of the month after your hire date.
The 401(k) plan matches 4% of salary and the match vests immediately.
Every employee has a learning budget of $1,500 per year for courses, books and conferences.

## Onboarding
Orientation is held on your first Monday. You receive your laptop on day one.
Mandatory security training must be completed within 14 days of your start date.

## Code of Conduct
Concerns can be reported to the ethics hotline at 1-800-555-0142 or ethics@northwind.example. Reports may be anonymous, and retaliation is strictly prohibited.

## Workplace Safety and Visitors
Business casual attire is the standard. Closed-toe shoes and safety glasses are required on the manufacturing floor.
Visitors must sign in at reception, wear a badge, and be escorted at all times in the lab.
